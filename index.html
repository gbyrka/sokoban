WAREHOUSE HANDBOOK

Everything in its place.

Move the warehouse keeper and push every wooden crate onto a diamond target. A placed crate turns green.

  • Use the arrow keys or W A S D on your keyboard.
  • On a touchscreen, tap the direction pad or swipe the board. Hold a pad button to keep moving.
  • You can push one crate at a time. You cannot pull crates or push them through walls.
  • A crate in a corner can get stuck. Use Undo / Z to rethink any move, Redo / Y to restore it, or Restart / R to start over.

No timer. No lives. Completed levels and records are saved on this device in a cookie for one year, renewed when you play. Your current position is also saved in browser storage. Clearing site data removes these saves.
